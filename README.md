# VectorViz

### 3D Vector Visualizer

An interactive playground for exploring **vectors in 3D space**.
Instead of looking at flat diagrams, you can grab a vector, move it around, and see how it behaves in real time.

---

### 🌟 What you can do

* **Manipulate vectors in 3D** → drag to change their length and direction, watch updates instantly.
* **Full camera control** → rotate, zoom, and pan to explore from any angle.
* **Clear visuals** → crisp arrows with heads/tails and a simple coordinate system.
* **Toggle details** → click a vector to show or hide its coordinates.
* **Manage vectors** → add, edit, or delete directly through the controls.
* **Reset view** → one click smoothly returns the camera to its starting position.
* **Switch modes** → flip between vector and point representations for different perspectives.
